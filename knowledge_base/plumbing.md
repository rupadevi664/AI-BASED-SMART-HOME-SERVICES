# Plumbing Knowledge Base

Service category: Plumbing

## Leaking kitchen tap

Problem: Kitchen tap is leaking, dripping continuously or pooling water around the base.

Possible causes:
- Damaged or worn rubber washer
- Loose connection between the tap and the water supply line
- Damaged valve seat or O-ring inside the tap body

What to check:
- Close the tap fully and see whether the dripping stops.
- Look under the sink for moisture on the supply line and shut-off valve.

Recommended service: Plumbing

Safety: Turn off the water supply at the shut-off valve before attempting any basic inspection. If water keeps flowing, shut the main valve.

## Leaking or burst pipe

Problem: A pipe is leaking, dripping through a wall or ceiling, or has burst.

Possible causes:
- Corroded or aged pipe sections
- High water pressure
- Loose or failed pipe joints
- Freezing temperatures cracking the pipe

What to check:
- Find the nearest shut-off valve and close it.
- Move electrical items away from the water.

Recommended service: Plumbing

Safety: Water near electrical outlets or appliances is dangerous — do not touch electrical panels while standing in water. Keep the supply off and wait for a professional for a burst pipe.

## Blocked or slow drain

Problem: Kitchen or bathroom drain is slow or fully blocked, water pools in the sink.

Possible causes:
- Grease or food waste build-up in kitchen sinks
- Hair and soap scum in bathroom drains
- Foreign objects stuck in the trap

What to check:
- Try a plunger gently on the affected drain.
- Avoid chemical drain cleaners repeated often — they can damage pipes.

Recommended service: Plumbing

Safety: Never mix chemical drain cleaners; toxic fumes can result. If one was used, do not add another product.

## Low water pressure

Problem: Water flow from taps or showers is much weaker than usual.

Possible causes:
- Partially closed shut-off valve
- Clogged aerator on the tap or showerhead
- Hidden leak in the supply line

What to check:
- Clean the aerator screen at the tap outlet.
- Compare hot and cold pressure to narrow down the cause.

Recommended service: Plumbing

Safety: A sudden pressure drop with visible damp patches may mean a hidden leak — book a plumber promptly.

## Water heater problems

Problem: No hot water, water is lukewarm, or the heater leaks.

Possible causes:
- Faulty heating element or thermostat (electric heaters)
- Pilot light failure (gas heaters)
- Sediment build-up in the tank
- Leaking tank or connections

What to check:
- Check the power supply or gas supply to the heater.
- Look for water around the heater base.

Recommended service: Plumbing (water heater repair)

Safety: Gas smell means leave the area and call a professional immediately — do not operate switches.
