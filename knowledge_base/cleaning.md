# Cleaning Knowledge Base

Service category: Cleaning

## Deep home cleaning

Problem: The house needs a full deep clean — floors, bathrooms, kitchen, windows.

What is included (typical):
- Bedroom, living room and balcony dusting, vacuuming and mopping
- Bathroom descaling and sanitising (tiles, WC, basin)
- Kitchen degreasing of counters, stove area and sink
- Window and glass cleaning, door and frame dusting

What to check before booking:
- Note the number of rooms and bathrooms.
- Mention any areas needing special attention (mould, heavy stains).

Recommended service: Cleaning

Safety: Professionals bring their own chemicals; keep children and pets away from freshly cleaned wet floors. Ensure ventilation while cleaning products are in use.

## Kitchen deep cleaning

Problem: Heavy grease on stove, chimney, tiles and cabinets; smells and sticky surfaces.

What is included (typical):
- Degreasing of the hob, backsplash and counter
- Chimney/exterior hood and filter degreasing
- Cabinet exterior wipe-down, sink and tap descaling
- Floor degreasing and mopping

Recommended service: Cleaning

Safety: Do not mix bleach and acid-based descalers — toxic fumes. Professional cleaners use the correct products for grease.

## Bathroom deep cleaning

Problem: Hard water stains, soap scum, mould in tile grout, smelly drains.

What is included (typical):
- Tile and grout scrubbing and descaling
- WC, basin and shower deep sanitisation
- Exhaust fan and drain cleaning
- Mould treatment on tiles

Recommended service: Cleaning

Safety: Mould areas should be ventilated; heavy mould may also need a leak check (see Plumbing).

## Sofa and carpet cleaning

Problem: Stains, dust, odours in upholstery or carpets.

What is included (typical):
- Vacuuming and stain pre-treatment
- Shampoo or foam cleaning, extraction and deodorising
- Drying guidance (keep windows open, avoid sitting until dry)

Recommended service: Cleaning

## General cleaning FAQ

- How long does a deep clean take? Usually 3–6 hours depending on rooms and condition.
- Do cleaners bring supplies? Typically yes — confirm equipment and chemical preferences when booking.
- How often? Regular cleaning weekly/fortnightly; deep cleaning every 3–6 months is typical.
