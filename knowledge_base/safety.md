# Safety Guidance Knowledge Base

## General safety rules for home problems

- When you smell gas: do not operate switches or flames, ventilate, leave the area, and call professionals from outside.
- When water is near electrics: switch off the relevant breaker first; never step in water to reach a panel.
- When you smell burning or see smoke from an appliance or switch: switch off the main power and book urgent help.
- For anything inside the distribution board, gas lines, refrigerant circuits or the roof: use licensed professionals only.

## Electrical emergencies

- Electric shock from any tap, appliance or fitting: stop using it immediately, switch off the breaker, and book an electrician urgently. Earthing may be faulty.
- Repeatedly tripping breaker: stop resetting it after one safe retry — repeated trips indicate a fault that needs an electrician.
- Sparking socket or warm faceplate: stop using the socket, turn the circuit off, book an electrician.

## Water emergencies

- Burst pipe: close the nearest shut-off valve or the main valve, keep electrics dry, and book a plumber urgently.
- Ceiling leak: place a bucket, puncture the bulge gently to drain safely if it is filling with water, and call a plumber — ceiling interiors may hold water.
- Sewage backup: keep children and pets away, do not use the affected fixtures, book a plumber.

## Gas safety

- Gas smell from the stove or geyser: close the cylinder/line valve, open windows, avoid every spark (no switches, no phone calls indoors), and call for help from outside.
- Yellow/orange stove flames instead of blue suggest incomplete combustion — book a technician to check the burner.

## When to wait for a professional (do NOT DIY)

- Anything involving refrigerant (AC gas), compressors, or microwave internals.
- Work inside the electrical distribution board or meter.
- Gas lines and geyser gas connections.
- Structural, roof or seepage repairs needing moisture diagnosis.

## If you are unsure which service you need

Describe the symptom to the AI assistant or pick the closest category from the Services page — the assistant suggests the most likely category, and verified experts can redirect to the right service on arrival. Emergencies (fire, gas, shock, flooding) should also be reported to local emergency services first.
