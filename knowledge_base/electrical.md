# Electrical Knowledge Base

Service category: Electrical

## Power switch making strange noises

Problem: A power switch or distribution board is buzzing, crackling, humming or making a strange noise.

Possible causes:
- Loose wiring connection inside the switch or board
- Faulty or worn switch mechanism
- Overloaded circuit humming under load
- Arcing inside a damaged switch — a serious fire risk

What to check:
- Listen whether the noise happens only when the switch is under load.
- Do NOT open the switch board yourself.

Recommended service: Electrical

Safety: Buzzing or crackling from switches or boards can indicate arcing. Switch off the circuit at the main breaker if safe to do so and book an electrician urgently. Never touch a switch with wet hands.

## Power outage in part of the house

Problem: One room or section of the house has no power while the rest works.

Possible causes:
- Tripped circuit breaker or RCD
- Blown fuse
- Faulty socket or wiring on that circuit

What to check:
- Look at the distribution board for a tripped breaker and reset it once.
- If it trips again, stop resetting and call an electrician.

Recommended service: Electrical

Safety: Repeatedly resetting a tripping breaker can overheat wiring. If a breaker trips repeatedly, book an electrician.

## Socket or outlet not working

Problem: A wall socket gives no power, sparks when plugging in, or feels warm.

Possible causes:
- Tripped breaker on that socket circuit
- Loose or burnt wiring behind the socket
- Damaged socket faceplate or contacts

What to check:
- Test the device in another socket to rule out the device.
- Feel whether the socket face is warm — warmth means stop using it.

Recommended service: Electrical

Safety: A sparking, warm or burning-smelling socket must not be used. Turn the circuit off and book an electrician.

## Frequent light bulb failures

Problem: Bulbs in one fitting blow frequently.

Possible causes:
- Loose wiring connection in the holder
- Bulb wattage too high for the fitting
- Voltage fluctuation on the circuit

What to check:
- Confirm the bulb wattage matches the fitting label.
- Check whether the bulb is loose in the holder.

Recommended service: Electrical

Safety: Let bulbs cool before touching. If a holder looks burnt or discoloured, stop using it and book an electrician.

## Electrical safety basics

- Water and electricity never mix — dry hands, dry floor.
- Never overload a single socket with multiple high-power appliances.
- Use a licensed electrician for any work inside the distribution board.
- Burning smell, smoke or shocks mean switch off the main power and call for urgent help.

Recommended service: Electrical
