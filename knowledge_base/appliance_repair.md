# Appliance Repair Knowledge Base

Service category: Appliance Repair

## Washing machine not working

Problem: Washing machine does not start, does not fill, does not drain, or will not spin.

Possible causes:
- Power supply or plug issue; tripped socket breaker
- Water tap closed or inlet filter clogged (no fill)
- Blocked drain pump or hose (no drain)
- Worn drive belt or lid switch fault (no spin)
- Door/lid not closing properly

What to check:
- Confirm the machine is plugged in and the socket works.
- Check the water tap behind the machine is open.
- Check for kinks in the inlet and drain hoses.

Recommended service: Appliance Repair

Safety: Unplug the machine before inspecting the back. Do not open the motor or capacitor area — book a technician.

## Refrigerator not cooling

Problem: Fridge runs but food is not cold, or the freezer stops freezing.

Possible causes:
- Thermostat set too high or faulty
- Dirty condenser coils (dust build-up)
- Door gasket leaking cold air
- Compressor or relay failure
- Overloaded fridge blocking air vents

What to check:
- Check the thermostat setting and door seal closure.
- Leave space between items for airflow; do not overfill.

Recommended service: Appliance Repair

Safety: Never attempt refrigerant or compressor repairs — certified technicians only. Keep the door closed as much as possible to preserve food.

## Microwave not heating

Problem: Microwave runs but food stays cold, or turntable/stops mid-cycle.

Possible causes:
- Faulty magnetron or diode
- Door interlock switch failure
- Control board issue

What to check:
- Test with a cup of water for 1 minute — if it stays cold, heating has failed.

Recommended service: Appliance Repair

Safety: NEVER run a microwave empty and never open the casing — the high-voltage capacitor can hold a lethal charge even when unplugged. Repairs are strictly for professionals.

## Air conditioner or geyser appliance faults

- AC and geyser faults are covered under AC Repair and Plumbing respectively, but the appliance technician handles installation and wiring of large appliances.

Recommended service: Appliance Repair

## General appliance safety

- Unplug before any inspection; never operate with wet hands.
- Burning smell, sparks or smoke: switch off and unplug, then book urgent repair.
- Do not DIY-repair anything with a compressor, gas, or heating element.
