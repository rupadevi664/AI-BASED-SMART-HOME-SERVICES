# AC Repair Knowledge Base

Service category: AC Repair

## Air conditioner not cooling

Problem: The AC runs but does not cool the room, or cooling is much weaker than before.

Possible causes:
- Dirty or blocked air filter restricting airflow
- Low refrigerant level or a refrigerant leak
- Dirty condenser or evaporator coils
- Compressor problem or failing capacitor
- Thermostat set or calibrated incorrectly

What to check:
- Check whether the air filter is blocked and clean or replace it.
- Make sure vents and airflow are not blocked by furniture or curtains.
- Check the thermostat mode and temperature setting.
- Listen for unusual noises from the outdoor unit.

Recommended service: AC Repair

Safety: Do not attempt to open the outdoor unit or handle refrigerant — refrigerant handling requires a certified technician. If cooling stays weak after cleaning the filter, book an AC service expert for inspection.

## AC leaking water indoors

Problem: Water drips from the indoor unit onto the wall or floor.

Possible causes:
- Blocked condensate drain line
- Dirty air filter causing the coil to freeze and then thaw
- Improperly sloped or disconnected drain pipe

What to check:
- Check whether the filter is dirty.
- Place a container under the drip and keep water away from electrics.

Recommended service: AC Repair

Safety: Keep water away from electrical outlets and switch off the unit until it is inspected if dripping is heavy.

## AC making unusual noises

Problem: Grinding, squealing, rattling or banging noises from the indoor or outdoor unit.

Possible causes:
- Loose panels or mounting screws (rattling)
- Worn fan motor bearings (grinding, squealing)
- Debris caught in the outdoor fan
- Compressor issues

What to check:
- Note when the noise occurs — starting, running, or stopping.
- Turn the unit off if the noise is loud or metallic.

Recommended service: AC Repair

Safety: Loud metallic or grinding noises mean stop the unit and book a technician — running it can cause bigger damage.

## AC turns on and off frequently

Problem: The AC short-cycles, turning on and off every few minutes.

Possible causes:
- Dirty filter causing overheating
- Oversized or undersized unit for the room
- Faulty thermostat
- Low refrigerant

What to check:
- Clean the air filter first — it is the most common cause.

Recommended service: AC Repair

Safety: Repeated short-cycling wears the compressor. Book a technician if cleaning the filter does not help.

## AC maintenance basics

- Clean or replace filters every 1–3 months of use.
- Keep the outdoor unit clear of leaves, dust and obstructions.
- Book professional deep-cleaning (coil + drain) once a year.

Recommended service: AC Repair (annual service)
