# Home Maintenance Knowledge Base

Service category: Home Maintenance / general

## Seasonal home maintenance checklist

Every 3 months:
- Clean AC filters; check the drain is not clogged.
- Run water through unused taps and drains to keep traps sealed.
- Check under sinks for early leak signs.

Every 6 months:
- Test all circuit breakers and RCD switches.
- Descale taps and showerheads (hard-water areas).
- Inspect door and window seals; lubricate hinges.

Yearly:
- Professional AC deep service (coils, gas pressure check).
- Water heater flush and inspection.
- Full electrical inspection of boards and earthing by an electrician.

Recommended service: Home Maintenance (or the specific category for each item)

## Damp walls and seepage

Problem: Patchy damp marks on walls or ceilings, peeling paint, musty smell.

Possible causes:
- Plumbing leak inside the wall or above the ceiling
- Roof or terrace seepage during rain
- Bathroom tile grout failure letting water behind
- Poor ventilation causing condensation

What to check:
- Note whether the patch grows after using water fixtures or rain.
- A moisture meter reading by a professional can separate leaks from seepage.

Recommended service: Plumbing (for leaks) or Painting/Waterproofing (after repair)

Safety: Damp near electrical wiring or switchboards needs an electrician's inspection before cosmetic repair.

## Pest control basics

Problem: Cockroaches, ants, termites or rodents in the kitchen or storage areas.

What helps:
- Sealing food in containers; fixing leaky taps (pests need water).
- Professional gel/chemical treatment for cockroaches and termites.
- Termite treatment is specialised — drill-and-fill by trained technicians.

Recommended service: Pest Control

Safety: Vacate the premises during chemical treatment as advised; keep food sealed and children/pets away until it is safe to return.

## Door lock and furniture carpentry

Problem: Door not latching, lock jammed, hinge squeaks, wardrobe door misaligned.

What helps:
- Lock cylinder lubrication (graphite, not oil).
- Hinge screw tightening; strike plate alignment.
- A carpenter handles warping, re-fitting and lock replacement.

Recommended service: Carpentry
